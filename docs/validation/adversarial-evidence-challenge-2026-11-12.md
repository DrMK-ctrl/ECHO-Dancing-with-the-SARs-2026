# Adversarial evidence challenge: scoped implementation

**State:** Implemented review workflow; not an autonomous causal evaluator.

## What the challenge does

`Challenge this finding` assembles a deterministic, read-only review from the active case assessment and its linked evidence. The API validates the assessment and its source records before returning the dossier. It keeps the observation, the selected hypothesis, analyst-recorded evidence relationships, competing explanations, linked sensitivity variants, unassessed evidence, and pre-recorded follow-up observations distinct. For T163, it also exposes the checksum-linked acquisition-condition review in a separate case-wide comparability lane when the selected hypothesis did not cite that review. The view labels that scope and does not convert it into directional evidence. It recomputes the displayed VV/VH polarization and DSWx optical-support threshold summaries from checksum-validated per-cell records, then exposes source-inspector links to the records and checksums. The optical support view includes a deterministic 50–95% exploratory sweep in five-percentage-point steps, calculated from retained per-cell values without choosing a preferred threshold. For T024, it also validates its three-acquisition temporal series against the exact linked pair and recomputes the adjacent-interval and first-acquisition-baseline comparisons from the recorded backscatter arrays on the all-date common support.

For a linked RTC-S1 pair, the challenge additionally verifies the pair-record checksum and identity, acquisition metadata and support counts. It recomputes the mean and median from the retained `values_db` array under the recorded valid and common-clear masks. It rejects a summary that does not reproduce. This checks arithmetic and record linkage; it does not establish product QA or validate an explanation.

Sensitivity summary recomputation likewise checks the statistics shown against their per-cell inputs; it does not rerun the original RTC-S1 raster calculation or the native-grid DSWx-HLS overlay. See the [sensitivity reproduction validation](adversarial-sensitivity-reproduction-2026-11-12.md) for formulas, observed T163 values, and limits.

## What it does not claim or calculate

- It does not infer a physical cause, validate analyst-authored source relationships, rank hypotheses, or produce confidence scores.
- It does not rerun the original raster or optical-overlay processing, alter measurement masks, or test evidence subsets. The T024 temporal-baseline view recomputes its retained three-date record; it does not assess alternate unrecorded acquisitions. For assessments without a validated linked series, temporal-baseline sensitivity is explicitly unavailable.
- It does not turn contextual reporting or optical products into validation of a specific SAR-derived boundary.
- It does not select or retrieve new observations. Follow-up recommendations are existing, recorded investigation plans, not an AI-ranked acquisition plan.
- Its geolocated SVG view is linked to the same checksummed SAR pair and optical-support record. A handoff now opens those same WGS84 cell measurements in the primary map workspace; the separate challenge-panel view remains available for source review.
- A reproducible summary is not equivalent to a scientifically accepted result; provider QA and case disposition remain controlling.
