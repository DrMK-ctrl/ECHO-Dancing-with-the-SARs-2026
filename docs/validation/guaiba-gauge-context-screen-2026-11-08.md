# Guaíba gauge context screen — T163-349925 — 2026-11-08

## Result

ANA/SGB's Usina do Gasômetro emergency gauge is about 669 m from the center of the T163 AOI and operated across both Sentinel-1 acquisition dates. It is a promising source for nearby hydrologic context, but it measures river level at one point; it cannot establish water extent within the SAR pixels or validate a SAR-derived flood boundary.

A contemporary published chart reproducing ANA/SEMA daily minima reports 5.00 m on 8 May and 3.51 m on 1 June 2024. These are secondary values and are not exact-time readings at the SAR acquisitions. ECHO therefore did not add them to the case evidence or use them to explain the backscatter change.

## Access and provenance findings

- The official SGB station map identifies the current Usina do Gasômetro gauge at `(-51.24194, -30.03472)`. The T163 AOI center is `(-51.235, -30.035)`.
- The public SACE CSV was retrieved and checksum recorded, but its 2,968 observations span only 2026-10-07 through 2026-11-07; it does not cover the 2024 acquisitions.
- One request to ANA's legacy `HidroSerieHistorica` endpoint for station `87444000` and 2024-05-08 through 2024-06-02 returned HTTP 200 with an XML “no data for station in requested period” error. The endpoint's response does not establish that the historical observations are absent.
- A follow-up screen queried both the raw emergency station (`87444000`) and corrected station (`87450020`) for 2024-05-03 through 2024-06-02, with both raw and consistent series modes. All four requests returned the same explicit no-data response for the relevant station code. ANA's service page says the legacy endpoint was extended only through 2026-06-30, redirected to a secondary database, and users should migrate to the new API. This makes the legacy endpoint an unsuitable archive check; its no-data response is not evidence that the source archive lacks the series. Exact requests and response checksums are in the [legacy endpoint recheck](../../data/research/ana-guaiba-legacy-endpoint-recheck-2026-11-08.json).
- ANA's official 2025 hydrology summary reports that station `87444000` remained above its flood stage for 30 days from 2024-05-02 through 2024-06-01. That establishes agency-reported regional gauge chronology overlapping the SAR acquisitions, but it is not the station's timestamped series and cannot validate water extent or SAR response at the AOI.
- ANA/SGB document that station `87444000` preserves the emergency readings from 2024-05-03 through 2025-08-29, while the corrected historical series is available as station `87450020` after a 1.18 m datum adjustment. The identifiers and vertical references must not be silently combined.
- ANA's current HidroWeb service documents authenticated telemetric-series access. The official ANA notice directs access requests to `hidro@ana.gov.br`; no credentials or access token were used or stored in this screen. The SGB public HidroSeries historical interface is another documented route, but its retrieval has not yet been verified.
- Follow-up verification used the official public HidroWeb station-inventory ArcGIS service and SGB HidroSeries portal. Exact station-code queries found zero current inventory entries for `87444000` and `87450020`; a `87450003` control returned one result, and the Guaíba-area query returned 11 stations. The inventory response does not establish whether historical observations remain available in the series archive. The SGB portal's global inventory search also returned no matches for the two 2024 codes. Exact queries, response hashes, and retained responses are recorded in the [inventory screen record](../../data/research/ana-sgb-guaiba-inventory-screen-2026-11-08.json). No series values were retrieved.
- Direct unauthenticated probes of ANA's current `dadosHistoricos` and `estacaotelemetrica` API routes returned HTTP 401 with the API's missing or malformed token message. This confirms that the current retrieval route requires authorization; it does not establish whether the requested observations are present. The probe metadata and responses are retained in the inventory screen record. No token was supplied, requested, or stored. ANA's [HidroWeb API access guide](https://www.gov.br/ana/pt-br/assuntos/monitoramento-e-eventos-criticos/monitoramento-hidrologico/orientacoes-manuais/manuais/manual-hidrowebservice_publica.pdf) describes its access process.

## Disposition

`PRIMARY_HISTORICAL_SERIES_NOT_RETRIEVED`. The official agency chronology confirms high river levels across both acquisition dates, but the machine-readable primary series remains unavailable through the checked legacy endpoint and current inventory. Current API retrieval requires an authorization token; the unauthenticated 401 response is an access restriction, not a dataset-absence result. The catalog misses do not prove that the historic series is absent from the archive. The gauge is contextual support: even a retrieved time series cannot validate the local SAR water mask or independently establish why backscatter changed. The next access route is authorized ANA API access or direct clarification from ANA/SGB about retrieval of the archived emergency-gauge series; unchanged legacy and inventory queries are closed.
