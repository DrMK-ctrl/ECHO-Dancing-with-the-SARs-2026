ECHO · ABOUT THIS VIEW
Investigation before conclusion
ASF catalog footprints show metadata coverage only. For a selected GCOV observation and geographic area, ECHO can retrieve and map a bounded backscatter measurement window with its mask, processing source, and provider QA disposition. A QA-held preview is diagnostic only; no change or event interpretation is implied.
ECHO demonstrates a source-linked diagnostic workflow. It has not established calibrated change-detection performance, damage assessment, physical cause attribution, or superiority to conventional methods; those claims require independent reference data and comparative validation.
The map backdrop can show NASA GIBS HLS Landsat reflectance browse imagery, MODIS Terra true color, or a NISAR Level-2 GCOV daily browse mosaic for the selected acquisition date. These are context layers, not the selected scene's quantitative raster or analysis input. The NISAR mosaic combines provisional GCOV observations and may mix modes, frequencies, and polarization compositions across areas; it is not a like-for-like time series. Transparent areas may indicate no displayed coverage.
We acknowledge the use of imagery provided by services from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). NASA GIBS documentation · NISAR GCOV Worldview guide
The map requests NASA GIBS imagery for its displayed bounds and selected observation date. Browse layers are separate from ECHO measurements and evidence calculations.
